Privacy Notice

Effective August 16, 2026

ALIVE is designed to make music visuals locally on your device. This notice explains the limited information involved when you use the site.

Your media stays in your browser

Your music and artwork are processed locally in your browser. ALIVE does not intentionally upload or store your music, artwork, filenames, song titles, artist names, waveform data, generated cover content, or exported videos. Video exports are created locally on your device. No account is required.

Temporary browser state

If you use share-to-remove, ALIVE stores a small session-scoped value in your browser so the b.com mark stays removed in that tab after a refresh. It contains no media or social credentials and normally ends when the tab’s browsing session ends. ALIVE does not verify whether you actually completed or published a share.

Hosting and analytics

Cloudflare serves this website and may process ordinary request and network metadata needed to deliver and protect it. When the configured Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon is enabled, ALIVE uses it for aggregate site traffic and performance information such as page views and loading performance. The integration is not given filenames, titles, artist names, artwork, audio, waveform data, generated covers, or exported videos. ALIVE does not use custom product-event tracking, advertising pixels, session replay, or marketing cookies in this release.

Choices and contact

You can block the optional analytics beacon with browser tools. Clearing the tab/session storage resets the clean-export unlock. For privacy questions, email .

No online service can promise perfect anonymity or security. ALIVE limits its collection and keeps the creative media workflow on-device.
